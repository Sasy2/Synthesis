How CGPA is calculated

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is a credit-weighted average. This means your grade points are multiplied by the credits for each course, added up, and then divided by the total number of credits you have taken. Note that Ashesi courses typically carry 1 or 0.5 credits.

Worked example of the calculator's method

Suppose your current CGPA is 3.0, with 20 credits completed. You have 15 credits remaining, and your target is a 3.3 CGPA:

  • points so far = 3.0 x 20 = 60
  • total credits = 35
  • points needed = 3.3 x 35 = 115.5
  • points still needed = 115.5 - 60 = 55.5
  • required average = 55.5 / 15 = 3.70
