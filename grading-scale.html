Minimum grades

Every course has a minimum passing grade. A course only counts towards graduation if the grade is at or above that minimum; otherwise, it must be retaken.

Credit load

A normal degree has 8 semesters, and a student can take a maximum of about 4.5 credits per semester.

How CGPA is calculated

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is a credit-weighted average. This means your grade points are multiplied by the credits for each course, added up, and then divided by the total number of credits you have taken. "P" (pass) grades do not count in the CGPA calculation.

Worked example of target calculation

Suppose your current CGPA is 3.0 after 20 credits, with 15 credits left, and a target CGPA of 3.3:

(3.3 x 35 - 3.0 x 20) / 15 = 3.70

Assumptions

This audit assumes a Computer Science degree on the standard Calculus track. Electives are treated as general slots. Summer semesters and repeated-course rules are not handled. Always confirm with your academic advisor.
