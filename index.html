Know where you stand before you see your advisor

Track your academic progress, plan your remaining semesters, and set realistic CGPA targets for your Computer Science degree.

Start my degree audit

How it works

  1. Select the grade you received for each course you have taken.
  2. Enter the number of semesters you have fully completed.
  3. Enter your target CGPA to see what average you need on your remaining courses.

What you will find out

  • Which courses you have successfully passed.
  • Which courses you still need to take, including any mandatory retakes.
  • How many credits you have completed and how many are left.
  • Whether you can graduate on time (maximum 4.5 credits/semester) and how to spread your credits.
  • Your current CGPA and the average required to reach your target CGPA.

Please note

This tool helps you prepare for your degree audit meeting and does not replace your academic advisor.
