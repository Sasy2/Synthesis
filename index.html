Plan your way to your target CGPA

Find out exactly what grades you need in your remaining courses to achieve your target Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA).

Start calculating

How it works

  1. Enter your current CGPA and the number of credits you have completed so far.
  2. Enter the number of credits you have remaining and your target CGPA.
  3. See the average grade you need and the possible grade combinations to reach your goal.

What you will find out

  • The exact average you need on your remaining credits.
  • Whether your target is achievable, already secured, or mathematically impossible.
  • The minimum letter grade you should aim for.
  • Different combinations of A, B+, and B grades that will help you reach your target.
